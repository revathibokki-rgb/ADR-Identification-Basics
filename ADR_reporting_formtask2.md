## 16. Final ADR Report Summary

A 28-year-old female receiving oral amoxicillin/clavulanate 625 mg three times daily for acute bacterial sinusitis developed a pruritic maculopapular rash on Day 4 of treatment. No other new medicines or relevant alternative exposures were identified. The suspected drug was discontinued, following which the rash gradually resolved. No rechallenge was performed. The reaction was classified as non-serious, with a **Naranjo score of 7 (probable)** and **WHO-UMC causality of probable/likely**.
